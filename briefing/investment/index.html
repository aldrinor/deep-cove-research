Capacity Guaranteed at Acceptance

Each installation is measured on site before go-live. The guaranteed figure is the contract number.

Recorded Kit sessions are replayed on BC's installed hardware at the contracted load. It passes only when 9 in 10 users stream at 100 tokens per second or more. No answer may fail.

Speed at the Guaranteed Load

  • Kit Ask: first words in 1 second or less for 95% of questions.
  • Streaming: 100 tokens per second or more for 9 in 10 users, about 19 times human reading speed.
  • Kit Think: its first progress line within 1 second.
Expert DetailHow Capacity Is Measured
  • Source. 220 independent measurements of this model on each GPU type, dated 5–13 November 2026.
  • Measured and guaranteed. Measured: the most simultaneous sessions at which 9 in 10 users stream at 100 tokens per second or more. The guaranteed figure uses 141 tokens per second or more on the same traces.
  • Clinicians. Clinicians in the busiest hour = guaranteed simultaneous streams × 16. At 8 questions in the busiest hour, halve the clinician figures.
  • Province. 10,888 simultaneous sessions at 204 tokens per second, measured at peak on 64 GPUs of one rack-scale system.
  • Named staff. At one in three named staff active in the busiest hour, Region serves about 74,000 named staff. BC has 16,438 physicians (CIHI, 2025) and 62,538 regulated nurses (CIHI, 2024).

Owning Compared with Renting

An entry-class AI server costs what 1.7 years of renting the same GPUs costs. A growth-class server costs what 1.4 years of renting comparable GPUs costs. Rented capacity sits in someone else's data centre and cannot have an air-gapped Core.

Expert DetailThe Payback Arithmetic
  • 8-GPU H200 at US$293,000 ÷ (US$2.52 per GPU-hour × 8 GPUs × 8,760 hours) = 1.66 years.
  • 8-GPU B200 at US$450,000 ÷ (US$4.65 per GPU-hour × 8 × 8,760) = 1.38 years.
  • Both compare the AI server price alone with rental of the GPUs alone. The Security Shell has no rental equivalent.

Questions from Finance and Procurement

What does Deep Cove cost?

C$1.09–1.26 million in hardware for Clinic. Hospital is C$2.44–2.96 million, Region C$4.80–5.85 million, and Province from about C$8.1 million plus the Security Shell. These are budgetary prices, November 2026. Software licence and support are per agreement.

What sets the low and high end of each range?

The November 2026 market price of the AI servers. Installation, integration, the acceptance test and staff training add 12% to the hardware.

Can a Clinic start on second-hand servers?

Yes, from a certified refurbisher that carries its own warranty. Same-generation servers keep Clinic's guaranteed capacity at C$0.95–1.03 million. Previous-generation servers start at C$0.83 million and serve about 130 clinicians.

What happens if a server fails?

From Hospital up, the other servers keep answering. 16 simultaneous streams continue at Hospital and 1,168 at Region. Clinic runs on one AI server, with a 4-hour on-site hardware service.

Can rural sites use Deep Cove without their own installation?

Yes. A Hospital or Region installation serves rural clinics over the health authority's private network. Questions never cross the public internet.

What does BC provide?

A room, power, cooling and a network connection. That means space for one to three racks, generator-backed hospital power and cooling for 16 to 84 kW. Clinic, Hospital and Region need no liquid cooling.

Kit is clinical decision-support software for licensed professionals. It shows its sources, and the clinician decides.

Image and document reading is non-diagnostic. Diagnostic imaging follows the Health Canada medical-device pathway.
