1

Patient Data over the Internet

Every question, along with any patient details in it, travels across the public internet to the AI company's computers.

Patient Data Within Your Walls

Questions travel over an encrypted connection to your own servers. Patient details never reach an outside AI company.

2

Login Open to the Internet

Anyone in the world can reach the sign-in page, so one stolen password can open a user's account from anywhere.

Your Own Secure Login

Users sign in through your own secure login with a password and a second step, such as a code on their phone. Unusual sign-ins set off an alert and can lock the account.

3

Shared AI Servers

Your questions run on the same computers as the questions of thousands of other customers. Security researchers keep finding ways to cross from one customer to another.

Your Own AI Servers

The AI runs on computers that only your organization uses. No other customer shares them.

4

Provider Staff and Contractors

The AI company's staff and contractors can reach the systems that hold your data.

Your Own IT Team

Only your own IT team manages the system, and no one can manage it remotely from outside. Sensitive steps need two people, and every action is logged.

5

Chat History on Their Servers

Every conversation is saved on the AI company's computers, under its rules for how long it is kept and who can see it.

Chat history locked per user

Conversations are saved on your own servers, and each person's chats are locked with their own key. The keys are kept in a hardware key safe, so not even an administrator can read the chats.

6

Third Parties

The AI company relies on other companies for hosting, storage and support, so your data can pass through businesses you have never heard of.

No Third Parties

No outside company handles your data. Only web search words leave your walls, and each search is checked first to make sure it contains no patient details.

7

Foreign Law

Most AI companies are based in the United States. US law can force them to hand over data, even data stored in Canada.

Canadian Law

Your data stays on your own servers in Canada, under BC and Canadian law.

8

Data Privacy, a Promise Only

The AI company promises not to train its AI on your data, but you have no way to check, and its terms can change.

Data Privacy by Design

Patient data never leaves your walls, and the system has no way to train the AI on your data. Your privacy does not depend on anyone's promise.

9

One Big Target

Data from many hospitals and companies sits in one place, which makes it a prize for hackers. One break-in can expose all of them.

Your Data Only

Your system holds only your organization's data. There is no shared pool of data from many organizations to attack.

10

Untested AI Updates

The AI company can change its AI at any time. An answer that was right yesterday can be different today, and no one at your organization tested the change.

AI updates tested on site

Every update is checked to confirm it really comes from Deep Cove, then tested on your own test server. It goes live only when you decide, and it can be undone in minutes.

11

Internet Down, AI Down

If the internet or the AI company goes down, your users lose the AI until service returns.

Internet down, AI still running

The AI runs within your walls, so it keeps working for everyone on your network. Only web search pauses until the internet is back.
