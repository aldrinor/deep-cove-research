Expert DetailLegal Basis
  • Foreign compulsion. The US CLOUD Act reaches data within a provider's “possession, custody, or control” (18 U.S.C. §2713).
  • BC privacy law. B.C. Reg. 294/2021 s.2 requires a supplementary assessment when sensitive personal information is stored outside Canada. Deep Cove stores it on site.

Questions from Officials

Where does patient data go, and who can see it?

It stays in the facility. Kit reads only what the signed-in clinician may already see. Deep Cove Intelligence has no remote access and holds no copy.

Is patient data used to train the AI?

No. An installation has no training system, and the agreement forbids any use of BC data for training.

Which AI model runs inside?

Deep Cove does not trust the model. It runs with no network, no credentials and no write path. Its identity is provided to the buyer under NDA.

How fast is it?

On the current hosted service, Kit Ask streams its first words in about 1.5 seconds. Kit Think finishes a hard medical question in about one to two minutes. Both were measured with test questions, no BC patient data.

How is it updated?

Only through the Delivery Bay. Each update arrives signed, is safety-tested on a machine BC owns and needs BC's sign-off. Rollback is one step.

What if Deep Cove Intelligence is no longer there?

Kit keeps working. There is no licence server and no remote off switch. The agreement includes a perpetual licence and source-code escrow.

What is Kit not for?

Kit does not make the diagnosis or prescribe. It lays out the options with their sources, and the clinician decides.
