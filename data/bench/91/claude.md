# Saint Seiya: Armor Classes, Warrior Classes, Power Tiers and Character Fates

**In short:**  Saint Seiya ranks its warriors by the armor they wear. The Bronze Saints are at the bottom, around Mach 1. Silver Saints run at Mach 2–5. Gold Saints, Marina Generals, the three Judges of Hades and the anime-only God Warriors all sit at light speed with the Seventh Sense. The gods are above everyone. The story works by having the five main Bronze Saints climb the whole ladder. By the end of Elysion they have the Eighth Sense and God Cloths, and they help kill Hades. Seiya survives, but in a coma under the curse of Hades’ sword.

## TL;DR

- **Power ladder (official framing):**  Bronze (~Mach 1) < Silver (Mach 2–5) < Gold / Seventh Sense (light speed) ≈ the seven Marina Generals ≈ the three Judges ≈ the anime God Warriors < Eighth Sense < God Cloth < gods in their true bodies.
- **Fates in the classic story:**  All twelve Gold Saints are dead by the end. Five die in the Sanctuary arc and come back as fake traitors in the Hades arc, and all twelve die again breaking the Wailing Wall. Only Marin and Shaina survive among the major Silver Saints. Of Poseidon’s generals, only Sorrento survives as a Mariner, and Kanon lives on until he dies in the Hades arc. Every Specter, Pandora, Thanatos, Hypnos and Hades die. All five main Bronze Saints survive, though Seiya is left near death.
- **Spinoffs change things:**
    - *Next Dimension*  (Kurumada’s own canon, 2006–2024) sends Athena back in time to save Seiya. It ends with Apollo erasing the heroes’ memories, and Kurumada’s sequel *Heaven Chapter*  began in May 2026.
    - *Omega* , *Soul of Gold*  and *Lost Canvas*  are separate, non-Kurumada continuities.

## 1. Introduction and the power system

**The franchise.**  Masami Kurumada’s manga ran in Weekly Shōnen Jump from December 3, 1985 to December 12, 1990 (the last chapter came out in V Jump). It has 110 chapters in 28 volumes. Toei adapted it as a 114-episode TV anime, aired on TV Asahi from October 11, 1986 to April 1, 1989. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya) *The Hades Chapter*  OVAs followed in three parts:

- Sanctuary: 13 episodes, 2002–03. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/ja/wiki/%E8%81%96%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB%E6%98%9F%E7%9F%A2_%E5%86%A5%E7%8E%8B%E3%83%8F%E3%83%BC%E3%83%87%E3%82%B9%E7%B7%A8)
- Inferno: 12 episodes, 2005–07.
- Elysion: 6 episodes, 2008. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya)

There have also been six animated films (1987–2014), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_films) a live-action film (2023) and the Netflix/Toei CGI series (2019–2024). [themoviedb](https://www.themoviedb.org/tv/90855-knights-of-the-zodiac)[wikiwand](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Knights_of_the_Zodiac:_Saint_Seiya) The manga had more than 50 million copies in circulation by 2022. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya)

**The premise.**  Orphans are sent around the world to become Saints, warriors of the goddess Athena, who has been reborn as Saori Kido. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters) Each Saint wears a living armor called a Cloth tied to one of the 88 constellations. [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Saint/1530812) Over three arcs they fight:

- the corrupted Sanctuary under Gemini Saga;
- Poseidon;
- Hades. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)

**Cosmo (小宇宙).**  Cosmo is the “inner universe”: the energy of the Big Bang present in every living being. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_Seiya)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists) A Saint’s strength is how far they can burn it.

**The senses.**

- **Seventh Sense (セブンセンシズ):**  total mastery of Cosmo. It is what makes a Gold Saint, and it gives light-speed movement and attacks. A Gold warrior “can circle the Earth seven and a half times in one second.” [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Seiya,%20the%20protagonist%20of%20the%20manga%20%22Saint%20Seiya%22./1494308)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_Episode.G_characters)
- **Eighth Sense (Arayashiki / 阿頼耶識):**  lets a person enter the Underworld alive and keep their soul. [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB) Shaka teaches it. Shiryū, Seiya and the Gold Saints at the Wailing Wall reach it.
- **“Infinite” or god-level Cosmo:**  wakes the God Cloths in Elysion.

**Speed benchmarks**  (repeated across the guidebooks and fan reference sites):

- Bronze Saints move at about the speed of sound.
- Silver Saints move at Mach 2–5. One Chinese summary of the official framing puts the gap between an ordinary Silver and a near-Gold warrior at “nearly a millionfold.”
- Gold Saints move at light speed. [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Seiya,%20the%20protagonist%20of%20the%20manga%20%22Saint%20Seiya%22./1494308)[baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Twelve%20Gold%20Saints/1509295)

**How the guidebooks place the enemy armies.**  Mostly this comes from *Saint Seiya Taizen*  (Shueisha, August 17, 2001):

- The Marina Generals are “comparable in rank and power to the Gold Saints.” [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Saints)
- The Scales are on paper as tough as Gold Cloths. [fandom](https://manga.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters) *Taizen*  explains that the Saints’ Cloths were first made specifically to counter the Scales, which is why Scales shatter so easily on the page. [saintseiyapedia](http://www.saintseiyapedia.com/wiki/Sanctuaire_sous-marin)
- The Specters of the Heavenly Stars, especially the Judges, are also ranked alongside the Golds. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Saints)[Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)
- The God Warriors appear only in the anime, so no Kurumada guidebook ranks them. The anime treats Siegfried as roughly Gold-level. [GameSpot](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/146044-saint-seiya-soldiers-soul/72646983)
- A god’s Kamui is far beyond any Cloth. Only a god in their true body can fully destroy a Gold Cloth. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Cloths)

**The tier scale used in this report:**

| Tier | Label | Benchmark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Bronze-level | ~Mach 1 |
| T2 | Silver-level | Mach 2–5 |
| T3 | Elite Silver / Bronze on the rise | Near light speed, partial Seventh Sense |
| T4 | Gold-level (Seventh Sense) | Light speed |
| T5 | Peak Gold / Eighth Sense | Light speed plus; can act in the Underworld |
| T6 | God Cloth | Can harm lesser gods |
| T7 | God / Kamui | Hades, Poseidon (fully awake) |

## 2. The armor classes

**Cloths (聖衣).**  Cloths are living armors made of orichalcum, gammanium and stardust sand. [shapes](https://shapes.inc/fandom/saint-seiya-knights-of-the-zodiac/cloths-and-constellations) There are 88: 48 Bronze, 24 Silver and 12 Gold. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Saints)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_Episode.G_characters) *Next Dimension*  adds a thirteenth Gold Cloth, Ophiuchus. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya:_Next_Dimension)[fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Cloths) Their main properties:

- They can “die” and be brought back with a Saint’s blood. Mu restores the Bronze Cloths with Gold Saint blood after the Sanctuary arc. These “New Cloths” turn gold whenever the wearer burns the Seventh Sense. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Shiry%C5%AB)[baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Seiya,%20the%20protagonist%20of%20the%20manga%20%22Saint%20Seiya%22./1494308)
- Gold Cloths freeze at absolute zero, survive Big Bang-level force, and need a god in their true body to destroy them completely. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Cloths)
- They answer to worthy wearers. Seiya repeatedly wears the Sagittarius Cloth, Shiryū the Libra Cloth, Hyōga the Aquarius Cloth, Shun the Virgo Cloth and Ikki the Leo Cloth. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)
- The **Libra Cloth**  carries twelve weapons that Saints may use only with the Libra Saint’s permission. Ikki uses the Libra spear on a Mammoth Pillar.
- The **Sagittarius Cloth**  is the prize of the Galaxian Wars, comes to Seiya’s rescue again and again, and marks him as Aiolos’s successor.

**Final Cloths and God Cloths.**  In the Hades arc, Shion uses Athena’s spilled blood to wake **Athena’s Cloth** , which sleeps inside the Athena statue in Sanctuary. He also soaks the Bronze Cloths in her blood. [fandom](https://manga.fandom.com/wiki/Athena_(Saint_Seiya)) Those Cloths can then cross the hyperdimension to Elysion; Specters such as Minos and Sylphid who try the same crossing are destroyed. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaSpecters)[Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB) In Elysion, Seiya’s god-level Cosmo wakes the blood inside the shattered Cloths and turns them into **God (Divine) Cloths** . These are close to Kamui and can defeat Thanatos and Hypnos, but Hades’ sword still pierces Seiya’s. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Pegasus_God_Cloth) Kurumada later called the God Cloths “excessive.” [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya)

**Black Cloths.**  Copies worn by Ikki’s Black Saints from Death Queen Island. The manga gives them a small role, and the anime expands it.

**Steel Cloths (anime only).**  Robotic armors built by Dr. Asamori with backing from Mitsumasa Kido. They draw no power from Cosmo. Shō wears the Sky Cloth (Toucan), Daichi the Land Cloth (Fox) and Ushio the Marine Cloth (Swordfish). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)

**Other anime-only armors.**  Docrates fights without a Cloth. The anime also adds the Crystal Saint’s Cloth and the Ghost Saints, a pair of different groups:

- the Caribbean bounty hunters led by Geist, from the TV anime;
- five resurrected former Silver Saints from the film *Evil Goddess Eris* . [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)

**God Robes (神闘衣, anime only).**  Worn by Odin’s God Warriors and named after the stars of Ursa Major. Each holds an Odin Sapphire. Gathering all seven wakes the **Odin Robe**  and the sword **Balmung** . [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)

**Scales (鱗衣).**  Orichalcum armors shaped like sea monsters, worn by Poseidon’s Mariners. Each of the seven Generals guards one Mammoth Pillar. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Poseidon)[shapes](https://shapes.inc/fandom/saint-seiya-knights-of-the-zodiac/cloths-and-constellations) Ordinary Mariner soldiers wear Scales that *Taizen*  reportedly ranks with Bronze Cloths. [goldsaintsanctuary](https://www.goldsaintsanctuary.fr/scales.php) Poseidon himself wears his own Scale/Kamui and carries the trident.

**Surplices (冥衣).**  Worn by Hades’ 108 Specters: 36 Heavenly Stars and 72 Earthly Stars. [fandom](https://manga.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters) Their power comes from Hades. Specters revived by Hades can be killed for good only after the barrier issue is dealt with; in *Lost Canvas* , Asmita makes a mala for exactly this. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya:_The_Lost_Canvas) The three Judges are the top tier. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Star_Specters) **Pandora**  commands the army for Hades. [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)

**Kamui (神衣).**  The armor of the gods themselves: Hades, Poseidon, Hypnos, Thanatos and Athena. Kurumada left the details deliberately vague. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya)[Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)

**Aries Mu**

- **Power level:**  Peak Gold. He has the strongest telekinesis of the Golds and is the only living Saint who can repair Cloths. [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Twelve%20Gold%20Saints/1509295)
- **Techniques:**  Starlight Extinction; Stardust Revolution; Crystal Wall; Crystal Net.
- **Key appearances:**  Repairs the Bronze Cloths. [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Twelve%20Gold%20Saints/1509295) In the Hades arc he defeats Deathmask, Aphrodite and Myu, and holds off Saga, Shura and Camus until Shion pins him. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)[baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Twelve%20Gold%20Saints/1509295) He fights Rhadamanthys at the castle. [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)
- **Fate:**  Dies at the Wailing Wall. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Saints)

**Taurus Aldebaran**

- **Power level:**  T4. Kurumada uses him repeatedly as the benchmark for showing off a new enemy (a “Worf”).
- **Techniques:**  Great Horn.
- **Key appearances:**  Seiya breaks his horn. In the anime, Syd knocks him out with Bud’s hidden help. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists) Sorrento weakens him with his flute before beating him. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints) He dies against Niobe at the start of the Hades arc. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Saints)[fandom](https://manga.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters) The OVA adds Europa, a village girl who likes him. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)
- **Fate:**  Killed by Niobe, but his final Great Horn had already cut Niobe apart, so they die together. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)[Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)

**Gemini Saga**

- **Power level:**  One of the strongest Golds. Saga is the Sanctuary arc’s final villain.
- **Techniques:**  Galaxian Explosion; Another Dimension; Genrō Maōken (Demon Emperor Illusion Fist).
- **Key appearances:**
    - Murders Shion and takes his place as Pope. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)
    - Tries to kill the infant Athena. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists)
    - Seiya defeats him using Athena’s shield, and Saga kills himself. [allthetropes](https://allthetropes.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya)
    - In the Hades arc he leads the “Wailing Trio” (Saga, Shura, Camus) as fake traitors. The three use the Athena Exclamation on Shaka, are brought before Athena, and die revealing they were loyal all along. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gemini_Saga)[fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Saints)
- **Fate:**  Dies twice, then again as one of the twelve at the Wailing Wall. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gemini_Saga)

**Gemini Kanon**

- **Power level:**  Close to Saga. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaMariners)
- **Techniques:**  Galaxian Explosion; Golden Triangle; Genrō Ken.
- **Key appearances:**  Saga imprisons him at Cape Sounion. He becomes Sea Dragon and manipulates Poseidon. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya) In the anime he also controls Hilda through the Nibelung Ring. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists) In the Hades arc he is redeemed: he kills a large number of Specters, including Rune, Phlegyas and four Specters wiped out with one Galaxian Explosion. [baidu +3](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Specter/1472039)
- **Fate:**  Takes off the Gemini Cloth, grabs Rhadamanthys and kills them both with Galaxian Explosion. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaMariners)[Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB) He is the only Gold Saint in the classic story who kills a Judge. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Wyvern_Rhadamanthys)[tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)

**Cancer Deathmask**

- **Power level:**  T4.
- **Techniques:**  Sekishiki Meikai Ha (Praesepe Underworld Waves).
- **Key appearances:**  His Cloth abandons him for his cruelty. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya:_Legend_of_Sanctuary) Shiryū kills him at Yomotsu Hirasaka. Revived in the Hades arc, Mu sends him back. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Capricorn_Shura) Rhadamanthys kills him at the castle. [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)
- **Fate:**  Dead. His soul joins the Wailing Wall sacrifice.

**Leo Aiolia**

- **Power level:**  T4–T5.
- **Techniques:**  Lightning Bolt; Lightning Plasma.
- **Key appearances:**  Saga puts him under the Genrō Maōken, and Cassios’s death frees him. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists) He kills three Silver Saints. In the Hades arc he kills Raimi and fights Rhadamanthys. [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)
- **Fate:**  Dies at the Wailing Wall.

**Virgo Shaka**

- **Power level:**  He is officially “the man closest to god” [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya:_Soul_of_Gold) and widely seen as the strongest Gold.
- **Techniques:**  Tenbu Hōrin (Treasures of Heaven), which strips away the five senses; Rikudō Rinne (Six Paths of Reincarnation); Tenma Kōfuku (Demon Pacifier); Kahn (barrier). [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)
- **Key appearances:**  Fights Ikki. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints) In the Hades arc he strips Saga, Shura and Camus of their senses, then lets them kill him with the Athena Exclamation so that he can enter the Underworld with the Eighth Sense. [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Twelve%20Gold%20Saints/1509295)[tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)
- **Fate:**  Killed at the Twin Sala Trees. [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Twelve%20Gold%20Saints/1509295) His soul joins the Wall.

**Libra Dohko**

- **Power level:**  T5.
- **Techniques:**  Rozan Hyaku Ryū Ha.
- **Key appearances:**  Survived the 18th-century Holy War [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya:_Soul_of_Gold) and sat at Rozan for 243 years with his aging slowed by Athena’s MISOPETHA-MENOS. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)[Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB) He regains his youth in the Hades arc. [myanimelist](https://myanimelist.net/featured/1128)
- **Fate:**  Dies at the Wailing Wall.

**Scorpio Milo**

- **Power level:**  T4.
- **Techniques:**  Restriction; Scarlet Needle, 14 needles in the shape of Scorpio, followed by the finishing Antares. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)
- **Key appearances:**  Beats Hyōga but lets him pass. Tests Kanon in the Hades arc. Fights Rhadamanthys at the castle. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)
- **Fate:**  Dies at the Wailing Wall.

**Sagittarius Aiolos**

- **Key appearances:**  Saves the infant Athena, is mortally wounded by Shura, and entrusts her to Mitsumasa Kido. [Wikipedia +2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters) In the manga he does not return with the other revived Golds; only his soul appears at the Wall. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)
- **Legacy:**  His Cloth repeatedly protects Seiya. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pegasus_Seiya)

**Capricorn Shura**

- **Techniques:**  Excalibur.
- **Key appearances:**  Killed Aiolos. Dies taking Shiryū’s Rozan Kō Ryū Ha [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Capricorn_Shura) and gives him Excalibur. In the anime he loses his arm in that fight. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Capricorn_Shura)
- **Fate:**  Dies again in the Hades arc.

**Aquarius Camus**

- **Techniques:**  Aurora Execution; Freezing Coffin.
- **Key appearances:**  Hyōga kills him at Aquarius. In the Hades arc Camus is revived, dies again at Hades’ castle, and Zelos kicks his body. [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)
- **Fate:**  Dies again in the Hades arc, then at the Wall.

**Pisces Aphrodite**

- **Techniques:**  Royal Demon Rose; Piranhan Rose; Bloody Rose.
- **Key appearances:**  Killed Daidalos. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaSilverSaints)[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_antagonists) Shun kills him. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andromeda_Shun) In the Hades arc Mu sends him back and Rhadamanthys kills him.
- **Fate:**  Dead. His soul joins the Wall.

**The Wailing Wall.**  The Wall cannot be broken by anything short of the light of the sun. All twelve Gold Saints, living and dead, gather there [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Saints)[myanimelist](https://myanimelist.net/featured/1128) and fire Aiolos’s arrow together. They die so the Bronze Saints can reach Elysion. [fandom](https://saintseiya.fandom.com/wiki/Gold_Saints)[Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB) Kurumada brought the five dead Gold Saints back as traitors because fans asked for it, and created Shion partly to show he was stronger than Deathmask and Aphrodite. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya)

## 5. Cross-class power comparison

**Official and textual statements:**

- Gold Saints, Marina Generals and the Judges share a tier. [fandom](https://manga.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Saint_Seiya_characters)
- Shaka is called “the man closest to god.” [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Seiya:_Soul_of_Gold)
- Orphée is said to be above Gold level. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaSilverSaints)
- Kanon is close to Saga and kills the most Specters. [tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaGoldSaints)[tvtropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/SaintSeiyaMariners)
- Only light-speed warriors survive inside the Underworld’s barrier at full strength, and only bearers of Athena’s blood survive the hyperdimension. [Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%86%A5%E9%97%98%E5%A3%AB)

**How the Bronze Saints end up:**  at T6. They are beyond any single Gold Saint once their God Cloths awaken, yet Hades in his true body still overwhelms them. That is the gap between Saint and god.

**Fan interpretation (not official):**

- Rankings of “strongest Gold” usually run Shaka ≥ Saga ≈ Kanon ≈ Dohko/Mu > the rest.
- Many fans see the Generals as weaker in practice than their billing. Poseidon woke early, so they were never at full strength. [baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Scale/3304747)
- Many fans also rate the God Warriors below the Golds except for Siegfried. [GameSpot](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/146044-saint-seiya-soldiers-soul/72646983)
- Aiolos’s legacy is felt through the Sagittarius Cloth and Seiya’s future as its Saint.

## Caveats

- **Technique names:**  English renderings differ between dubs and reference wikis.
- **Unconfirmed claims:**  I could not check two points against the guidebook text itself: that *Taizen*  calls Marin the strongest female Saint, and that it ranks ordinary Mariner Scales with Bronze Cloths. Treat both as reported.
- **God Warrior rankings**  come from the anime and from fans, not from Kurumada.
- **Specter details**  (stars, techniques, defeats) rely mainly on Japanese Wikipedia’s Specter article, which cites *Taizen*  and the character data in volume 28. The English star names are literal translations of the kanji.
